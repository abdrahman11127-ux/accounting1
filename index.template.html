How to use

  1. Use the cards above to switch workspaces.
  2. Each system has a single cash form – choose Cash In or Cash Out, enter the amount, and (optionally) pick the party involved.
  3. When moving money between systems select the linked business so the other ledger updates automatically.
  4. Liaqat & Sons has a dedicated widget inside Personal Savings to track the receivable separately from Depalpur.
  5. Every ledger can be filtered by supplier / party and exported to PDF for quick sharing (e.g. the Zubair Bhai ledger in the Game Business tab).
